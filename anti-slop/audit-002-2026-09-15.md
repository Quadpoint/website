# Antislop audit 002

Scope: current home page against the supplied `Downloads/mockups/website` section references.

Design read: QuadPoint’s supplied references establish a navy environment with electric-blue structure, a limited orange action accent, and concentric-Q geometry. ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 1.

1. HIGH, R-17 and R-38: The requested return of dashboard numbers cannot be presented as current business data without a verified source. Use clearly labelled *illustrative preview* values, or supply approved figures and their source.

2. HIGH, R-25: The home-page color override changes heading and section-label colors globally after each component renders. In several combinations the blue label text sits on a similarly dark-blue surface, losing the intended separation. Establish a high-contrast light label treatment or use white headings consistently on the navy sections.

3. MEDIUM, R-31: The hero still uses blue and white CTAs, while the reference reserves orange for the primary action and uses a restrained transparent secondary control. The navbar’s “Get Started” CTA is also blue. Make orange the deliberate action color and document it as the limited conversion accent.

4. MEDIUM, R-04 and R-31: Solutions uses blue-on-blue icon tiles and the featured orange tile uses a navy icon. The supplied reference calls for white icons on blue and orange tiles, with orange marking the AI service. Apply that semantic distinction consistently and do not add unrelated decorative glyphs.

5. MEDIUM, R-10, R-12, and R-14: The solution, product, process, and values cards remain predominantly flat. Introduce a restrained glossy surface and a single hover elevation treatment on actionable cards only, preserving readable text and avoiding blur or glow across every card.

6. MEDIUM, R-05: The AI workflow section retains its low-contrast blue icon treatment and the process cards have no icons. The reference uses orange workflow glyphs and meaningful, distinct process icons to make the sequence scannable. Add only icons that map directly to Discover, Design, Build, and Grow.

7. MEDIUM, R-05 and R-20: The concentric-Q motif exists in the hero and CTA, but it is too faint and isolated to provide the section-level identity shown in the references. Extend the motif selectively to the hero edge and final CTA panel, with enough contrast to read as brand structure rather than as background noise.

8. LOW, R-03 and R-35: Any addition of a large Q motif, glass highlight, or hover translation requires verification at phone and tablet widths. Ensure decorative geometry cannot create horizontal overflow and retain non-hover feedback for touch devices.

## Decision rationale for approved fixes

- Navy gives the B2B software story a continuous, focused environment.
- Orange is limited to primary conversion actions and AI emphasis, making the action hierarchy immediate.
- White-on-colored icon tiles make the service and process symbols legible at a glance.
- A restrained glossy highlight provides depth on interactive cards without turning the page into a glassmorphism treatment.
- Concentric-Q geometry ties the layout to QuadPoint’s supplied logo rather than generic decoration.

## Follow-up

The owner approved findings 1 through 8, with the explicit instruction that standard section labels use white text on a light-blue surface distinct from the dark-navy background.

- Dashboard values now appear only inside an explicitly labelled illustrative preview.
- Primary conversion actions use orange, while the hero’s secondary contact action is transparent with a white border.
- Standard labels use white text on `#2469be`; content headings remain white on the navy page surfaces.
- Solution, workflow, process, and value icons are white on blue or orange tiles. Process numbers and product links use orange.
- The card finish is limited to a glossy inset highlight and a pointer-device hover lift. The CTA gains a brighter blue panel and a retained Q-mark.
- `npm run lint` passed. `npm run build` passed, including TypeScript and static generation for all 13 routes.
