# Antislop audit 001

Scope: current home page compared with `Downloads/mockups/website/website-mockup.png`.

Design read: B2B technology landing page for business owners, using the supplied navy and electric-blue brand language, dial ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 1.

The reference is a valid style direction, not a template to copy. Its dark blue surfaces, concentric-Q motif, and orange action color can be used as the site’s own visual system because they derive from QuadPoint’s supplied mark and business context.

1. HIGH, R-17 and R-38: The dashboard visuals show business figures and activity states as though they are live data (`$4,820`, `148`, `2,341`, `4 workflows running`, and similar values). The source does not establish these as real product data. Replace them with a clearly labelled illustrative preview or use a product image containing verified data.

2. HIGH, R-26: The contact form reports “Message Sent” after logging form data in the browser. It does not deliver a message. Connect it to a real endpoint or change the visible result so it honestly states that delivery is not yet available.

3. MEDIUM, R-31: The project has the ingredients of the supplied visual direction, including navy surfaces, brand blue, circular Q geometry, and orange calls to action, but the reasons for those decisions are not recorded. Add a compact design rationale when implementing the mockup so the darker presentation, spacing rhythm, and limited accent use remain intentional instead of spreading across every section.

4. MEDIUM, R-05 and R-14: The current home page repeats centered heading, supporting copy, and equal card-grid compositions across Trust, Solutions, Process, Portfolio, and Why QuadPoint. The reference uses a more varied rhythm: a compact category row, a three-column solution comparison, product cards, a workflow split, then a process strip. Recompose sections around the existing content hierarchy rather than applying one card treatment everywhere.

5. MEDIUM, R-04 and R-13: Several solution and value cards rely on the same thin Lucide icon treatment and blue icon tiles. Match the reference’s more selective orange and blue emphasis only where it signals priority, and let text or the Q motif carry the remaining hierarchy. Do not add decorative icons or glows merely to fill empty space.

6. MEDIUM, R-35 and R-03: The supplied mockup is desktop-only, while the existing site includes multiple grids, a fixed header, and a mobile drawer. Before shipping the reference-inspired redesign, verify the layout at phone, tablet, and desktop widths, including no horizontal overflow, 44px touch targets, and a readable mobile navigation state.

7. LOW, R-25 and R-32: The site has a visible global focus outline, but text with translucent white values on navy surfaces and focus states have not been measured against the final backgrounds. Check contrast after the redesign and retain a visible keyboard focus treatment on every link, button, and the mobile menu controls.

## Approved implementation record

The owner approved findings 1, 3, 4, 5, 6, and 7. Finding 2 remains intentionally unresolved at the owner’s request.

- Navy surfaces create a continuous environment for the business-software story and distinguish the home page from generic white-card marketing layouts.
- The supplied blue Q-mark and electric blue carry structure, navigation, and hierarchy; orange is limited to the AI & Automation solution so it signals the featured service group.
- The home page moves from services to products, then workflow, process, and principles, which follows the reference’s narrative rather than repeating a single centered-card pattern.
- Existing product mockups remain as visual concepts, but each visible dashboard now identifies itself as illustrative rather than reporting unsupported live data.
- Motion is reduced to purposeful entrance transitions already used by the site; perpetual activity and scroll animations were removed from the hero.
- Responsive grids retain their existing stack points, and the mobile navigation control now has a 44px minimum target.
